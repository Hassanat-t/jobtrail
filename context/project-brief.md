# Project brief

**Name:** JobTrail (working name)
**Owner:** Anidu Hassanat
**Type:** Personal portfolio project

## Pitch
A personal CRM for your job search: save jobs in one click while browsing, track each one from application to offer, and see what's working.

## The problem
Job seekers apply to dozens of roles and:
- lose track of where they applied and when
- forget to follow up
- can't tell which job boards or approaches actually get replies

Spreadsheets work but are tedious: copying links, typing dates, no reminders, no stats.

## Who it's for
- Primary: early-career developers actively job hunting (including the owner)
- Secondary: anyone applying to more than ~10 jobs at once

## Goals
1. Saving a job takes under 5 seconds (via the Chrome extension).
2. Every application has a clear status and history.
3. Nobody forgets to follow up (reminder emails).
4. Users can see their response rate and best sources.

## Non-goals (for now)
- Reading the user's inbox automatically (Gmail integration is a possible later level)
- Job search or job recommendations
- Team or recruiter features

## Success for the portfolio
- Deployed with a live URL
- Owner uses it for their own job search and can quote real numbers
- Clean README with screenshots, tests and CI
